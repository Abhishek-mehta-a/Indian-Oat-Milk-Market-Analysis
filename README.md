# 🥛 Indian Oat Milk Market Analysis - Power BI Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview
This repository contains an end-to-end interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze the rapidly growing Indian Oat Milk market. It was developed as a comprehensive data analytics project (MBA Semester 4 Major Project) to track market size, evaluate brand performance, break down unit economics, and forecast demand over a 3-year horizon.

## 📈 Key Market Insights
* **Total Market Size:** ₹332.3 Cr (with a 39.34% YoY Search Surge).
* **Market Leader:** *So Good* dominates with a 64% market share.
* **Unit Economics:** At an MRP base of ₹310/L, the Net Realization is ₹220/L with an EBITDA margin of 10.6%.
* **Growth Projection:** The market is projected to grow at a ~45.9% CAGR, reaching ₹373 Cr by 2029.

## 📑 Dashboard Structure
The dashboard is divided into three analytical views:
1. **Market Overview & Brand Analytics:** Tracks Total Market Size, YoY Search Surge, Brand Market Share (Donut Chart), and Channel Volume Mix Breakdown.
2. **Enterprise Unit Economics & Cost Breakdown:** Features a dynamic Waterfall chart detailing Gross Margins, CAC & Marketing costs, and COGS internal component splits.
3. **3-Year Enterprise Demand Sensing (2026-2029):** Highlights Volume Forecasts, Target ASP, and a detailed Year-on-Year growth matrix across B2B and Q-Commerce channels.

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills Demonstrated
* **Tool:** Power BI Desktop
* **Skills:** Data Modeling, DAX (Data Analysis Expressions), UI/UX Dashboard Design, Financial Cost Breakdown, Demand Forecasting, and Business Analytics.
